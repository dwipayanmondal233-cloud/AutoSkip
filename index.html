AutoSkip

// auto-click skip buttons. no root.

↓ Download APK

Android 8.0 or newer. Always the latest build.

Features

  • Watches the screen and taps Skip buttons for you.
  • Works in YouTube, browsers, games and more.
  • Your own keywords: add or delete them.
  • Master switch to pause without turning off the permission.
  • Live skip counter.
  • Only scans when the screen is on. Saves battery.
  • No data collected. No internet needed.

Install steps

  1. Tap Download APK and open the file.
  2. Allow "Install unknown apps" if asked.
  3. Open AutoSkip, tap Open Accessibility Settings.
  4. Find AutoSkip, turn it on, and allow.
  5. Go back to the app. Status should say ACTIVE.
  6. Settings, Apps, AutoSkip, Battery, choose Unrestricted.

Problems and fixes

"App blocked to protect your device"
Play Store, profile, Play Protect, gear, turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect". Install the app. Turn it back on.
Accessibility switch is greyed out
Settings, Apps, AutoSkip, ⋮ menu, "Allow restricted settings". Then turn on the service.
Stopped working after an update
Turn the Accessibility service off and on again.
"App not installed" when updating
Debug builds change signature. Uninstall the old version first, then install.
"This site can't ask for your permission"
An app is drawing over the screen (floating bubble, recorder). Stop it first, or grant the permission manually in Settings.
Chrome camera or permission blocked
Fix it in Chrome site settings.
Stops after a while
Your phone is killing background apps. Set Battery to Unrestricted for AutoSkip.
A Skip button is missed
Find its exact text with Google's Accessibility Scanner app. Add that text as a keyword in AutoSkip.

Honest limits

  • Ads change often. Some will be missed, especially on YouTube.
  • Buttons with only an icon and no text cannot be matched.
  • Banking apps and secure screens block accessibility apps.
  • Only exact button text is matched, on purpose.
  • Not on the Play Store. Play Protect may warn you.
